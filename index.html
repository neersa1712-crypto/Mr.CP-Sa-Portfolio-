Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — a simple business site costs less than a multi-page site with bots, dashboards or database integration. Message me on WhatsApp with your requirement for an honest quote.

How long does a project take?

Simple sites typically take a few days; bots, SaaS and custom software take longer depending on complexity. I'll give you a realistic timeline after understanding your requirement.

Do you build websites from scratch or redesign existing ones?

Both. I can build a new site from the ground up or improve, redesign and re-optimise an existing website.

Can you build bots and SaaS applications?

Yes — custom bots for automation/notifications, and SaaS products with authentication, dashboards, APIs and databases.

Is my website secure?

I follow security best practices — HTTPS, hardened response headers, safe form handling, and no unnecessary third-party scripts — to keep sites hardened against common attacks.

Do you work with clients outside Rajasthan?

Yes — I work with clients across India and internationally, entirely remotely via WhatsApp and email.
